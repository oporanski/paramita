Our breeds

Persian

A calm companion who picks the sofa over the kitchen counter. Needs daily brushing; that long coat will not look after itself. For someone who wants a cat who is present but not loud.

Exotic Shorthair

The Persian temperament in a short coat: the same gentleness, a fraction of the grooming. A good choice if you love the Persian type but have no time to brush every day.

Maine Coon

Large, calm and sociable — follows the household from room to room. Keeps growing for up to four years, so for a long while you have a kitten in a big body. Needs space and attention.

English Setter

A dog built for movement, not for a flat without walks. Gentle with people and children, but needs a proper run every single day. The silky coat needs regular combing.

American Cocker Spaniel

Cheerful, attached to people, eager to work with you. Ears and coat need constant care — this is not a low-maintenance dog. Happiest in a family that spends time together.
