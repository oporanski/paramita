About us

My name is Gosia, and this is my cattery and kennel — small, run from home, welfare first. Our animals don't live in cages or runs; they are simply part of the family.

Cats and dogs share our house. What matters to me is that they are happy and healthy.

My cats are registered with the Baltic Feline cat club, my dogs with the FCI.
